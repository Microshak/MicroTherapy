# DBT — Dialectical Behavior Therapy

## When to Use
- Emotions are overwhelming ("I completely lost it")
- Client needs immediate coping skills
- Emotional dysregulation is the primary concern
- Impulsivity is present
- Intense anger or relationship conflict
- Client needs distress tolerance techniques

## When NOT to Use
- Client wants deep insight work (use CBT or ACT)
- Client is stable and wants growth/planning (use SFBT)
- Immediate crisis (use Crisis Response)

## Core Techniques

### STOP (Distress Tolerance)
- **S**top — freeze, don't react
- **T**ake a step back — breathe
- **O**bserve — what's happening inside and outside?
- **P**roceed mindfully — what's the wise choice?

### TIPP (Crisis Survival)
- **T**emperature — cold water on face (dive reflex)
- **I**ntense exercise — short burst
- **P**aced breathing — slow exhales
- **P**aired muscle relaxation — tense and release

### DEAR MAN (Interpersonal Effectiveness)
- **D**escribe the situation factually
- **E**xpress your feelings using "I" statements
- **A**ssert what you want clearly
- **R**einforce why it's beneficial
- **M**indful — stay on topic
- **A**ppear confident
- **N**egotiate — be willing to give to get

### Check the Facts
- What is the emotion? What triggered it?
- What are the facts? What are my interpretations?
- Am I assuming a threat? How likely is it?

## Key Principles
- Two things can be true at once (dialectics)
- Skills over willpower — you need tools, not just trying harder
- Build a life worth living
