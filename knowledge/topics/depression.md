# Depression

## Common Presentations
- Persistent low mood or emptiness
- Loss of interest in previously enjoyed activities
- Fatigue, low energy, "heavy" feeling
- Negative self-talk, guilt, worthlessness
- Hopelessness about the future
- Sleep and appetite changes
- Difficulty concentrating

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- When did you last feel like yourself? What was different?
- What's one small thing that still brings you any spark?
- What does the depression tell you about yourself? Is it telling the truth?
- What's been lost that you're grieving?

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| CBT | Behavioral activation, challenging negative thoughts |
| ACT | Values-based action despite low mood |
| General Therapy | When energy for structured work is low |
| SFBT | Finding exceptions — moments when depression lifts |
