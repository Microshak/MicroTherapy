# Anxiety

## Common Presentations
- Racing thoughts, what-if spirals
- Physical symptoms: tight chest, shallow breathing, restlessness
- Avoidance of anxiety-provoking situations
- Catastrophizing: assuming worst-case outcomes
- Perfectionism as anxiety management
- Social anxiety: fear of judgment or rejection

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- What's the worst that could happen? And then what?
- How likely is that outcome, honestly?
- What would you tell a friend who had this worry?
- When has this fear been wrong before?
- What's the cost of the avoidance?

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| CBT | Examining catastrophic thoughts, testing predictions |
| ACT | Accepting anxious feelings without fighting them |
| DBT | Distress tolerance when anxiety peaks |
| General Therapy | First disclosure, validation |
