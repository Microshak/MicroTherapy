# Self-Esteem

## Common Presentations
- "I'm not good enough" narrative
- Impostor syndrome at work or in relationships
- Constant comparison to others
- Difficulty accepting compliments
- Perfectionism as proof of worth
- Basing self-worth on achievements or others' approval

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- Where did you learn that your worth depends on what you achieve?
- Who benefits when you believe you're not enough?
- What would be enough? Can you picture it?
- If your best friend described themselves the way you describe yourself,
  what would you say to them?

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| CBT | Examining evidence for/against self-critical beliefs |
| ACT | Defusion from "not enough" story, values-based living |
| SFBT | Building on strengths and past successes |
| General Therapy | Validation, exploring origins |
