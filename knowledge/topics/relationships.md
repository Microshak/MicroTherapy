# Relationships

## Common Presentations
- Recurring conflict patterns with partner
- Trust issues (difficulty trusting, or being trusted)
- Communication breakdowns
- Attachment concerns (anxious, avoidant, disorganized)
- Boundary setting difficulty
- Decisions about staying or leaving

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- What's the pattern? When does this fight happen, and what's it really about?
- What are you afraid would happen if you said what you really feel?
- How did the people who raised you handle conflict? Love? Trust?
- What need isn't being met — and have you asked for it directly?

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| General Therapy | Venting, validation, exploring patterns |
| DBT | Interpersonal effectiveness, DEAR MAN |
| CBT | Examining assumptions about partner's intentions |
| ACT | Values clarification — what kind of partner do you want to be? |
