# Neurodivergence

## Common Presentations
- ADHD: time blindness, executive dysfunction, rejection sensitivity
- Autism: sensory overwhelm, social exhaustion, masking
- Late diagnosis — grief and relief mixed together
- Feeling "broken" before understanding neurotype
- Difficulty with systems designed for neurotypical brains

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- What would your day look like if the world was built for your brain?
- What's one accommodation that would make a real difference?
- How much energy goes into masking? What would happen if you did it less?

## Important: Neurodiversity-Affirming Approach
- **Do NOT** treat neurodivergence as a problem to fix
- **DO** validate the real challenges without pathologizing the person
- **DO** focus on environment changes, not "fixing" the person
- **DO** acknowledge the strengths that come with different wiring

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| General Therapy | Validation, exploring identity, grief around late diagnosis |
| ACT | Acceptance, values, reducing struggle with executive challenges |
| DBT | Emotional regulation (RSD), distress tolerance |
| SFBT | Practical strategies, building on strengths |
