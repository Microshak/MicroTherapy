# Life Transitions

## Common Presentations
- Career changes: job loss, retirement, promotion stress
- Relationship transitions: marriage, divorce, empty nest
- Parenthood: new parent adjustment, infertility, deciding whether to have kids
- Aging: health changes, mortality awareness, role shifts
- Relocation: loss of community, starting over
- Identity shifts: "Who am I now?"

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- What are you leaving behind? What are you moving toward?
- What's the hardest part of this transition for you?
- Who were you before this change? Who do you want to become?
- What would make this transition feel meaningful rather than just hard?

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| General Therapy | Exploring mixed feelings, grief for what's lost |
| ACT | Values clarification — what matters most in this new chapter |
| SFBT | Goal-setting, building momentum in the new situation |
| MI | Ambivalence about making the change |
