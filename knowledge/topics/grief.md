# Grief

## Common Presentations
- Death of a loved one
- Non-death losses: divorce, job loss, health decline, lost opportunities
- Ambiguous loss: person still present but changed (dementia, addiction)
- Anticipatory grief: dreading a future loss
- Disenfranchised grief: loss not socially acknowledged
- Guilt about "not grieving right" or "moving on too fast"

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- Tell me about them. What do you miss most?
- What's the hardest time of day?
- What would they want you to know right now?
- What's one thing you wish people understood about your grief?

## Important: Grief-Sensitive Approach
- **Do NOT** try to fix, reframe, or find silver linings
- **DO** make space for the pain. Grief needs witness, not solutions.
- **DO** normalize: "There's no right way to grieve. There's no timeline."
- **DO** invite stories and memories

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| General Therapy | Primary — presence, validation, witnessing |
| ACT | Integrating loss into a life worth living |
| CBT | If guilt or self-blame becomes distorted |
