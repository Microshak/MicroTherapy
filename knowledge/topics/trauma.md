# Trauma

## Common Presentations
- Intrusive memories, flashbacks, nightmares
- Hypervigilance, always scanning for threat
- Emotional numbing or dissociation
- Avoidance of trauma reminders
- Difficulty trusting others or feeling safe
- Complex trauma from childhood or prolonged abuse

## Key Therapeutic Questions
- (First:) Do you feel safe right now, in this moment?
- What helps you feel grounded when things get intense?
- What happened to you — not what's wrong with you?

## Important: Trauma-Informed Approach
- **Safety first.** Always. Ask about current safety.
- **Do NOT** push for details of traumatic events. Let the client lead.
- **DO** focus on present-day impact, not event reconstruction
- **DO** offer grounding techniques if client becomes dysregulated
- **Be aware:** This system is NOT equipped for intensive trauma processing.
  Know your limits. Refer to human therapist for trauma-focused work.

## Modality Guidance
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| General Therapy | Validation, safety, stabilization |
| DBT | Distress tolerance, grounding, emotion regulation |
| ACT | Acceptance of trauma responses without being controlled by them |
